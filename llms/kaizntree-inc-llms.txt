# Kaizntree Inc.

> Kaizntree is a New York-based operations platform for growth-stage consumer packaged goods (CPG) brands. Kaizntree OS is a cloud ERP for purchasing, inventory, sales, fulfillment, production, and lot-level traceability across raw materials, intermediate goods, packaging, and finished/bundled products (with COGS, margins, multi-location, batch codes, and expiry dates). JP is an ERP-agnostic AI operations assistant that automates purchase-order processing, reporting, data sync, and OTIF tracking. Backed by 500 Global (seed, 2022). No public developer API, OpenAPI, or SDK is published at this time.

## Products
- [Kaizntree OS](https://os.kaizntree.com/): Cloud ERP for CPG operations — inventory, purchasing, sales, fulfillment, production, traceability.
- [JP — AI Ops Assistant](https://kaizntree.com/ai-ops): ERP-agnostic automation for high-volume operations workflows.
